Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Publisher/brand: Calyxar

PC Guard is a Windows desktop security application intended to help the device owner monitor local security activity. This policy describes the information processed by the currently tested desktop functionality, user choices, and important limitations. It applies to PC Guard Free and planned Pro features insofar as they are available.

1. Information PC Guard processes

  • Facial recognition: approved facial feature templates from enrollment and appearance updates, along with camera frames processed during identity checks.
  • Security records: locally generated event history, Windows session events, warnings, device status, and troubleshooting logs.
  • Images: saved security snapshots when a feature or security event creates them.
  • Preferences: selected camera, monitoring intervals, privacy retention settings, and application configuration.

2. How the information is used

PC Guard processes this information to help identify authorized people, detect or record local security events, show alerts, maintain monitoring preferences, and produce reports requested by the device owner.

3. Storage and security

In the tested Windows desktop implementation, encrypted Face Vault templates are stored locally and protected using Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI), tied to the relevant Windows account. PC Guard's security snapshots, exported reports, logs, and event records are not necessarily encrypted by PC Guard itself. Windows security, permissions, and disk encryption may provide additional protection depending on device configuration.

PC Guard's core desktop features do not require a separate Calyxar or PC Guard online account. Windows account authorization is required for certain sensitive facial-profile management operations in the tested implementation.

4. Data transmission and third-party services

The desktop monitoring functions tested during development operate locally. The developer has not represented that all Windows, Microsoft Store, device-driver, or other third-party services operate without network connections. When Microsoft Store purchasing is available, Microsoft may process purchase and licensing information under Microsoft's own terms and privacy practices. This website itself does not request account details or payment information.

Future cloud synchronization, mobile remote access, diagnostics, or analytics features would require a review and update to this policy before their release.

5. Your choices, retention, and deletion

  • Turn PC Guard protection on or off. Stopping protection is designed to end its monitoring process and camera checks.
  • Manage enrolled facial profiles, including appearance updates and profile deletion, subject to Windows authentication for sensitive changes.
  • Preview and delete saved snapshots, and set a snapshot-retention preference.
  • Use Delete All My Data to remove data within the application's defined local reset scope after authorization and confirmation.

Local deletion does not automatically remove reports previously exported, separately created backups, synced files, Windows or third-party records, or data stored beyond the reset's defined scope. Ordinary file deletion also does not guarantee forensic erasure from SSDs or backups.

6. Other people and minors

Device owners are responsible for using camera monitoring and facial enrollment with appropriate authorization, providing any legally required notice or consent, and complying with applicable laws where PC Guard is used. PC Guard is not intended as a service for collecting children's biometric data without appropriate legal authority.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PC Guard's capabilities or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when updates are published.
